    Can I take more than one Gomz formula?

    Yes. Daily Multi plus one focused formula is the most popular pairing, and the doses are set so two formulas together stay within safe daily levels. If you take other supplements or medication, check with your GP or pharmacist first.

    Are they suitable for children?

    Gomz is formulated for adults 18 and over. Because they taste like sweets, keep the pouch out of reach of young children.

    Are Gomz vegan?

    Yes. We use fruit pectin instead of gelatine, plant-based colours from fruit and vegetable concentrates, and a carnauba wax coating so they don't stick together.
