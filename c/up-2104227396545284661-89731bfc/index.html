A quiet ridge, five minutes from Main Street.

Room for everyone on one of Senoia's most private streets, with a screened porch that looks straight into the hardwoods.

Set back on a wooded lot, this four-bedroom home pairs an open kitchen and family room with a main-level primary suite and a flexible bonus room upstairs. Wide-plank floors run through the main level, and the kitchen island seats five.

Out back, the screened porch steps down to a stone patio and a level, fenced yard. Downtown Senoia's shops and restaurants are an easy drive, and Peachtree City's golf-cart paths are close by.
