Rebuilding ordering and invoicing for 900 trade customers.

A UK food wholesale network took orders by phone, email and a slow web shop, then retyped them into accounts. We designed and built one platform for ordering, credit and invoicing, and kept the old shop running until the last customer had moved.

The situation

Restaurants, cafés and caterers ordered for next-morning delivery by phoning a sales desk, emailing lists, or using a web shop that did not know their prices or their credit limit. Every order was retyped into the accounts system by hand.

Wrong prices, missed lines and late invoices led to about 120 disputes a month. The finance director wanted one system the customers would actually use, without forcing anyone to change overnight.

What we inherited

  • A WooCommerce shop with one price list, while every trade customer had their own.
  • An on-premise accounts system with a nightly CSV export as its only interface.
  • Delivery slots planned in a shared spreadsheet per warehouse.
  • Credit limits checked by phone with the finance team before large orders.

What we did, year by year

  1. 2021

    Discovery with twelve customers

    Two weeks at the sales desk and in twelve customers' kitchens, mapping how orders really arrive and where they go wrong.

  2. 2021 — 2022

    Design and pilot

    Ordering, credit and delivery-slot screens designed with the pilot group; first orders placed on the new platform in month five.

  3. 2022

    Accounts integration

    A two-way sync with the accounts system: customer prices and credit in, invoices and payments out, reconciled every fifteen minutes.

  4. 2023

    Customers moved warehouse by warehouse

    The old shop stayed live alongside the new platform until the last customer moved, nine months later.

  5. 2024 — today

    Mobile ordering and credit control

    A mobile ordering flow for kitchen staff, automatic credit holds and releases every week or more often.

Inside the product

Built for a kitchen at 22:00 and a finance team at 09:00: the same order, two views, no retyping.
