What we do for a product that already exists.

You don’t need another agency to start from zero. You need a team that can read what is there, keep it running and move it forward without breaking what your customers rely on.

  • Codebase takeover

    We audit the code, infrastructure and documentation you have, write down what we find in plain language, and take responsibility for it — including the parts nobody wants to touch.

    • Technical due diligence
    • Architecture review
    • Handover from previous teams

  • Web and mobile engineering

    New features, rebuilds and migrations for web platforms and iOS and Android apps, released in small steps behind a regression suite so your users never feel the change.

    • React and Node.js
    • Laravel and PHP
    • Flutter and React Native

  • Product and interface design

    Design for dense, working software: dashboards, admin tools, multi-role workflows. Designed with the people who use it every day, built by the same team.

    • UX research
    • Design systems
    • Brand identity

  • Technical consultancy

    A senior second opinion on a rewrite, a hire, a vendor or a roadmap. We tell you what we would do with your money if it were ours.

    • Roadmap and estimates
    • Cloud cost review
    • Security and access

How a takeover runs.

The same four stages on every engagement, drawn to scale. You always know what is happening, what it costs, and what comes next.

Week 0Week 2Month 1Month 2Month 3Year 1 and on
  1. 1

    Audit

    Weeks 1–2

    Read access to code, servers and tickets. We map the system, its risks and its debts.

  2. 2

    Stabilise

    Week 3 to month 3

    Monitoring, backups, a staging environment and a CI/CD pipeline before any new feature.

  3. 3

    Plan

    Month 3, alongside stabilising

    A roadmap agreed with your product owner, estimated in weeks and reviewed every month.

  4. 4

    Ship and maintain

    From month 4, for years

    Releases twice a week, an on-call rotation and an SLA. You see every change in the demo.
